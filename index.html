Reed Naidoo

PhD Researcher, The Institute of Cancer Research · Research Scientist, Sentinal4D

Cell shape is a functional readout, not just a proxy. I build models that learn how 3D cell morphology responds to genetic and chemical perturbation, then use them to predict, simulate and interpret those responses.

Research

Models that treat the cell's form as data worth predicting.

Generative virtual cells

Diffusion models that simulate how 3D cell morphology changes under perturbation, and what those changes reveal about mechanism.

Cross-modal translation

Linking cell images to transcriptomics and other modalities, so imaging can stand in for costlier assays.
